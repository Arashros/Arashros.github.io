My undergraduate thesis examined age-related trends in anatomical measurements of the right cerebral hemisphere. I analyzed structural MRI morphometric data from more than 600 subjects using the Harvard–Oxford cortical atlas, under the supervision of Dr. S. Abolfazl Valizadeh.

This thesis established my foundation in MRI-derived anatomical data analysis and motivated my continued interest in biomedical engineering and neuroimaging.
