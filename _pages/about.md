I am a Biomedical Engineering graduate from Shahid Beheshti University with a focus on biomedical data analysis. My undergraduate thesis examined age-related trends in right-hemisphere anatomical measurements derived from structural MRI data.

My project work combines Python-based data analysis and machine learning with an interest in practical medical instrumentation.

## Research interests

- Structural MRI and brain morphometry
- Biomedical signal processing
- Reproducible machine-learning workflows for biomedical data
- Medical instrumentation and clinical engineering

## Selected work

- [Brain Age Prediction](/portfolio/brain-age-prediction/): grouped evaluation of age-regression models using structural MRI morphometric features.
- [ECG Beat Classification](/portfolio/ecg-arrhythmia-classification/): an in-progress project on annotated ECG beat classification using participant-aware evaluation.
